# Einvoice

A free web app that makes an invoice or receipt on a phone and saves it as a PDF or an image. No sign-in needed.

## Pages

- `index.html` - home page: why invoices and receipts help, and what Einvoice does
- `how-it-works.html` - the six steps, tips and common questions
- `app.html` - the invoice and receipt maker
- `site.css` - styles shared by the home and how-it-works pages

Open `index.html` in a browser to start.
