How Einvoice works

Six small steps. You can do the whole thing in about 2 minutes.

  1. 1

    Choose Invoice or Receipt

    Tap Invoice if you want your customer to pay you. Tap Receipt if they have already paid. You can switch at any time.

  2. 2

    Add your business

    Type your business name. Your phone number and address are optional. To put your logo on the page, tap Add your logo and pick a picture from your phone.

  3. 3

    Add your customer

    Type your customer's name and phone number if you want them on the page. You can skip this step.

  4. 4

    Add what they bought

    For each item, type the name, how many, and the price of one. Tap Add another item for more. The amounts and the total update as you type.

    Pick your money type (Naira, Dollar, Pound, Euro, Cedi, Shilling or Rand), and add a discount if you want to give one.

  5. 5

    Check the number and date

    Einvoice fills in the next number and today's date. Change them if you need to. On an invoice you can add a pay-by date. On a receipt you choose how they paid. You can also add a note, such as your bank details.

  6. 6

    Make it and save it

    Tap Make my invoice (or Make my receipt) and look at the page. If it is right, tap Download PDF or Download Image. If you see a mistake, tap Change something, fix it and make it again.

Good to know

  • Not sure where to start? Tap Fill in an example at the top of the form to see how a finished page looks.
  • Next time is faster. Your business name, phone, address, logo and money type are remembered on your phone, so you only add the items.
  • Numbers count up by themselves. Invoices go INV-001, INV-002 and so on. Receipts go RCT-001, RCT-002. You can type your own number if you prefer.

Questions people ask

Is it free?

Yes. Making invoices and receipts is free.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Open the page and start.

Where are my details kept?

On your phone, in your browser. Einvoice does not send what you type to a server and does not keep a copy of your invoices. If you clear your browser's data or switch to another phone, you start fresh.

Can I use it on a computer?

Yes. It works in any modern browser, on a phone, a tablet or a computer.

My download did not start. What now?

Tap the download button again. If your browser asks where to save the file, choose a place you can find later, such as Downloads or Files.
