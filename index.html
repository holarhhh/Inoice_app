Why every seller needs an invoice or receipt

A piece of paper with the amount on it saves you time and trouble. This is what it does for you.

Get paid with less chasing

An invoice shows the amount and the date to pay. Your customer knows exactly what to do, so you have to ask less often.

Fewer arguments

The items, the prices and the total are written down. Nobody has to remember what was agreed.

Proof for both sides

A receipt shows that your customer has paid. It protects you if someone says they never paid, and it protects them too.

A record of your sales

Keep your invoices and receipts and you can see what you sold. That helps at the end of the month, and whenever someone asks you to show proof of your business.

Invoice or receipt?

They look alike, but you give them at different times.

Invoice

Give it before you are paid. It asks your customer to pay, and says how much and by when.

Receipt

Give it after you are paid. It shows that you received the money and carries a PAID stamp.
